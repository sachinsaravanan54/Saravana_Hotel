Our Story & Heritage

Founded over a decade ago, Sri Saravana Hotel has been the heartbeat of authentic South Indian cuisine in our community. We started with a simple dream — to bring the true flavours of Tamil Nadu to every table.

From the golden crispiness of our Dosas to the comforting warmth of our Full Meals served on a banana leaf, every dish is a celebration of tradition, prepared fresh daily using time-honoured recipes passed down through generations.
